## 🧠 About Me

I'm **Elvis Melchor Carbajal Dall'Orso** — a Systems Engineering student at **Universidad San Juan Bautista** (Ica, Perú) with a clear mission: become a **Deep Learning Researcher** who designs, trains, and improves AI models from first principles.

Not the person who just connects APIs — the one who understands the math, reads and writes papers, reproduces results, and pushes ideas forward.

> **My destination:** any frontier lab — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, xAI, Mistral, or wherever serious research happens.  
> **My approach:** rigorous foundations → entry-level data work (funds the journey) → DL built from scratch → Transformers/LLMs at research level → research life (papers, reproduction, interpretability) → formal bridges (fellowships & residencies).
